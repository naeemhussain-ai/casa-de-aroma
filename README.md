# Aura Atelier

I am providing these PDF files as reference:

- home1.pdf

- shop1.pdf

- catagory1.pdf

- about1.pdf

- faq1.pdf

- contact1.pdf

Study the PDFs carefully to understand the business, products, content, categories, information architecture and customer journey.

IMPORTANT: These PDFs are REFERENCE ONLY. Do NOT copy their visual design, exact layout, colors, typography, spacing or section composition.

Build a completely NEW, highly premium SINGLE-PAGE luxury fragrance/perfume e-commerce website that looks at least 10X better than the reference.

The final website should feel like:

LUXURY FRAGRANCE + HIGH-END FASHION EDITORIAL + MODERN PREMIUM E-COMMERCE.

It must immediately feel expensive, sophisticated, exclusive and professionally designed by a top luxury digital agency.

==================================================

DESIGN DIRECTION

==================================================

Create a completely different art direction from the reference.

Use a premium DARK theme:

- Obsidian / near-black as primary

- Dark charcoal

- Soft ivory

- Champagne / muted metallic gold as a small accent

Suggested:

#0B0B0A

#151513

#F3EFE7

#C6A46A

Do NOT make the website overly gold.

Use elegant high-contrast serif typography for major headlines and a clean modern sans-serif for UI/body text.

Use large editorial typography, cinematic imagery, sophisticated spacing, asymmetrical layouts, subtle borders, premium shadows and refined visual hierarchy.

Avoid generic Shopify/template designs, excessive rounded cards, cheap gradients and clutter.

==================================================

SINGLE PAGE STRUCTURE

==================================================

Create one beautiful long-form homepage with smooth navigation:

1. Announcement Bar

2. Premium Sticky Navigation

3. Cinematic Hero

4. Featured Collections

5. Best Sellers

6. Editorial Brand Story

7. Fragrance Discovery

8. Signature Product Feature

9. Why Choose Us

10. Testimonials

11. FAQ

12. VIP Newsletter

13. Premium Footer

Every section must feel intentionally designed and visually connected.

==================================================

HERO

==================================================

Make the hero extremely impressive and cinematic.

Full-screen fragrance campaign with a premium perfume bottle, dramatic lighting, dark environment, reflections, shadows and subtle atmospheric effects.

Use a sophisticated headline such as:

"SCENT, REFINED."

Supporting copy:

"Exceptional fragrances curated for those who leave an impression."

Buttons:

"EXPLORE THE COLLECTION"

"DISCOVER YOUR SCENT"

Add an elegant scroll indicator.

Do NOT create a boring centered hero.

==================================================

==================================================

- Quick Add

Product hover should feel premium: subtle image zoom, secondary image where appropriate and elegant information transitions.
